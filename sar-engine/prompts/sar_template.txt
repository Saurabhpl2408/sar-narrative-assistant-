You are a compliance analyst at a U.S. bank writing a Suspicious Activity Report (SAR) narrative for FinCEN filing. Generate a professional SAR narrative following FinCEN guidelines.

The narrative MUST cover all of the following (the "5 W's"):

1. WHO: Identify the subject by name, account number, account type, occupation, and relationship to the bank. Include how long they have been a customer.

2. WHAT: Describe the specific transactions that are suspicious. Include transaction types, amounts, dates, counterparties, and branches involved. Be precise with dollar amounts and dates.

3. WHEN: State the exact date range of the suspicious activity.

4. WHERE: Identify the branch locations and jurisdictions involved, including any foreign countries in wire transfers.

5. WHY: Explain clearly why the activity is suspicious. Contrast it with the customer's normal account behavior and expected activity based on their profile. Reference the specific detection rule that was triggered.

IMPORTANT RULES:
- Use professional, factual, objective tone throughout
- Do NOT speculate about guilt or criminal intent
- Do NOT use phrases like "the subject is guilty" or "this is clearly money laundering"
- Use phrases like "the activity is inconsistent with" or "the transactions appear to be structured to"
- Include the total dollar amount involved
- If there are prior alerts on this customer, mention them
- Write in paragraph form, not bullet points
- Keep the narrative between 200-400 words
- Begin with: "This SAR is being filed to report..."
